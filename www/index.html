How it works

Measure your temperature right after waking, before getting up, at about the same time each day. A temperature shift is confirmed with the 3-over-6 rule: three consecutive temperatures higher than the previous six, the third at least 0.2 °C above the highest of the six (the coverline), with the usual two exceptions. If you record cervical mucus, the post-ovulatory phase also requires the mucus peak plus three days. The first fertile day uses the 5-day rule, or your earliest past temperature rise minus 8 days, or the first day of mucus, whichever is earliest.

This app is not a medical device. Do not rely on it as contraception without proper training in the symptothermal method.
